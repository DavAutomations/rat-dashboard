Data notes & calculation methods

One workbook row is one reported sighting, not a count of individual rats. All headline metrics and charts use the full set of selected rows. The “All Boroughs” total includes four records with unspecified boroughs; the five-borough bar chart excludes those four. Population differences and reporting behavior affect counts.

Closed percentage = records with Status “Closed” ÷ all selected records. Average resolution = mean elapsed days from Created Date to Closed Date among Closed cases with valid, nonnegative intervals. Records lacking a usable close date are excluded from that average, not treated as zero. Dates stored as spreadsheet dates are preserved; text dates are parsed day-first. Source dates are not independently corrected.

Monthly counts follow Created Date. June 2018 is partial and July–December 2018 are unavailable. Ordinary least squares fits January 2010–May 2018 (101 complete months), with month number as the only feature. The dashed line extends to June 2019. This is a simple historical trend projection, not a validated forecast or confidence interval. The slope is the change in expected monthly reports per elapsed month, not a percentage.

ZIP circles count all selected records with a valid five-digit ZIP. Positions are the mean of valid sighting coordinates within each ZIP, not postal centroids or ZIP boundaries. ZIPs without usable coordinates cannot be placed. Pin view is a deterministic sample of up to 750 geocoded cases per borough; it does not display every case. All sample details are embedded. Coordinates outside the NYC bounding extent or missing coordinates are excluded from the map only.

The offline map uses simplified geographic outlines from NYC Open Data / Department of City Planning. No online tiles, fonts, scripts, or services are loaded. Current outlines provide geographic context for historical reports.
